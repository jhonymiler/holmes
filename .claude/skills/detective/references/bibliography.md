# Bibliography

Sources of the method. Read the original before citing a detail that is not in the references.

## Evidence evaluation and forensics
- ENFSI, *Guideline for Evaluative Reporting* (2015) — LR, hierarchy of propositions, verbal scale.
- PCAST, *Forensic Science in Criminal Courts* (2016) — error rate, 95% bound, validity.
- NAS, *Strengthening Forensic Science* (2009) — sources of error and bias.
- DOJ OIG, Mayfield case (2006) — the closest non-match, the discrepancy rule.
- Dror (2020); Dror & Kukucka, LSU-E (2021) — sources of bias, cascade, order of information.
- UK FSR-G-217 (2020); College of Policing, APP Investigation.
- RFC 3227 — order of volatility in collection.

## Digital forensics and cryptanalysis
- NIST SP 800-86; Carrier, thesis (2006) — indirect observation is a hypothesis.
- SWGDE 12-Q-001 — systematic error, second method.
- Berkeley Protocol (2022) — open-source collection and verification.
- Friedman, *Military Cryptanalysis I*.
- Lasry, thesis (2018) — scorers, effective unicity.
- Oranchak, Blake, Van Eycke, solution of the Z340 (2024); von zur Gathen (2021).

## Analysis and judgment
- Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis* (1999).
- CIA, *Tradecraft Primer* (2009); ICD 203 and 206; Kent (1964).
- Platt, "Strong Inference" (1964); Chamberlin (1890).
- Heuer & Pherson, *Structured Analytic Techniques*.
- Quick Wins (2013); PHIA (2025); ATP 2-33.4; Red Team Handbook.
- Pólya, *How to Solve It*.
- The Friedmans, *The Cryptologist Looks at Shakespeare* (1955) — acceptance criteria.
- McKay et al., "Solving the Bible Code Puzzle" (1999); Gelman & Loken (2013) — fine-tuning.
- Mandel et al. (2018); Chang et al. (2018) — evidence for and against the techniques.
- Tetlock, *Superforecasting* — outside view, calibration.
